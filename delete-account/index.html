Delete Your Account

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

Visa Run, developed by Jhayography (Dr. Rolando Gaspar Jr.), lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly within the app. This page explains how to do it and exactly what is removed.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Visa Run app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account and data are removed immediately. This action cannot be undone.

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently and immediately removes all of the following:

  • Your account and login credentials (email address)
  • All saved trips, stops, and travel history
  • Photos you uploaded (stop photos and profile avatar)
  • Profile information (name, nationality)
  • Purchase and subscription tier records

What data is kept

Nothing is retained after your account is deleted. There is no additional retention period. Once the deletion completes, your data is permanently erased from our systems.

Note: records of past in-app purchases held by Google Play or Apple are governed by those platforms' own policies and are outside Visa Run's control.

Can't access the app?

If you are unable to open the app or sign in, you can request account and data deletion by email. Include the email address associated with your account so we can locate and remove it.
